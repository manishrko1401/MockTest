Given below is a set of five sentences that, when properly sequenced, form a coherent paragraph. Arrange the sentences in the correct sequence and answer the questions that follow.

A. Farmers ferment cocoa beans in wooden boxes, where spontaneous environmental microbes significantly influence flavour profiles.

B. They discovered microbial communities evolve across stages, with bacteria showing “memory” while fungi often arrive externally.

C. Chocolate, like yogurt, kimchi, and sauerkraut, is a traditional fermented food transformed by diverse microbial activity.

D. These findings may enable designing controlled starter cultures, allowing chocolate flavours similar to modern cheese or beer.

E. Scientists monitored cocoa bean fermentation in Colombia, tracking changes in temperature, pH, and microbial dynamics.

Which of the following is the FIRST sentence of the coherent paragraph?

Solution

The paragraph discusses cocoa bean fermentation, highlighting how microbial activity shapes flavour and how scientific monitoring may guide future flavour control in chocolate production.

Sentence C is the first sentence as it introduces the broad idea that chocolate, like other foods, is a fermented product shaped by microbial activity.

Sentence A follows as the second sentence, narrowing the focus to how farmers ferment cocoa beans in wooden boxes and how microbes influence the flavour.

Sentence E comes next as the third sentence, showing how scientists studied the fermentation process in Colombia by monitoring temperature, pH, and microbial dynamics.

Sentence B is the fourth sentence, presenting the findings that microbial communities evolve during fermentation, with bacteria retaining “memory” and fungi arriving from external sources.

Sentence D concludes the paragraph by highlighting the implications of these findings, namely the possibility of designing controlled starter cultures to refine chocolate flavours.

The correct sequence of the given sentences to form a coherent paragraph is CAEBD.

Answer: C

Therefore, the correct answer is "Sentence C."
